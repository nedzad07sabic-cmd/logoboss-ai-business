Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay online to request a website?

No. Send your project inquiry first. Scope, price and delivery details are confirmed before an order is finalized.

Are appointments automatically confirmed?

Not necessarily. An appointment request can remain pending until your business approves it. The final workflow depends on your requirements.

Is AI calling included automatically?

No. Phone numbers, AI providers and any required integrations must be configured for your project. Additional provider fees may apply.
