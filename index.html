        Frequently asked questions · Häufige Fragen

        How long does setup take? / Wie lange dauert die Einrichtung?

        The delivery time depends on the selected package, content and integrations. We confirm the scope and timeline before work begins.

        Do I have to pay online?

        No. Your website request is sent first. LogoBoss Web AI contacts you to confirm the order and delivery details.

        Can I upgrade later?

        Yes. You can start with a smaller package and add individual services or upgrade later.

        Does it work in German and English?

        Yes. The LogoBoss Web AI offer supports German and English customer-facing experiences where configured.

        Are there additional AI or phone costs?

        External AI providers, phone numbers, calls and SMS can have separate usage charges depending on the provider.

        Can the system be adapted to my business?

        Yes. Services, booking flows, content and automations can be configured around the business requirements.

        Clear & transparent
